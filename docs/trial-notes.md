## arena · 2026-09-22 · combat practice on the fixture server · in progress

Three runs a drill; a death anywhere fails it; the medians are what the next change has to beat. `sh .test-combat/start.sh &` then `MC_PORT=25574 node scripts/arena.js`. A watching player is opped, put in spectator and snapped onto the bot at every drill.

Unmeasured against skeletons, which is where it should pay.
16. *Recovering in the open is not recovering.* With a shooter in view and health under eighteen, the hunt now digs in rather than dozing where it stands.

**Standing still is a bug (2026-09-22 19:40 UTC).** Every stall found today had one shape: a step returned having done nothing, or a wait was chosen with nothing else on offer, and the next tick landed in the same place. A harvest chore failing silently twenty times a second; a sleep refused for a block on the bed, then a night behind a wall; a Nether food gate with the chest lid blocked and the animal search resting, spinning at twenty passes a second. So the rule is now structural rather than a patch per case.

55. *Stillness is checked, not hoped against* (`src/stillness.js`). Activity is what the world can see: a block moved, a dig, a pickup, a blow landed, the pockets changed. Twenty seconds without any, outside a short list of waits worth making (asleep, a fight, holding a door), is a stall. It is logged with what the bot was on, and both the work loop and the idle loop hand it a detour: work that can be done from here (the idle chores, ore in reach, a look around, the night mine), Jev's pick, three minutes each. A detour that fails rests for five minutes; a rung that stalls three times in ten minutes waits its turn. The idle loop's "rest" option is gone.
56. *The night is mined, not waited out.* With nothing watching the pocket and no bed to sleep in, the bot digs a mine from the shelter: toward ore in view of the rock, or down to a working depth and along a branch, one staircase step a tick. A tunnel in rock is as closed as the pocket. A mob that shows is the survival layer's; a usable bed comes first; at dawn the mine is left for the day's work.
57. *The Nether food gate always acts.* The chest, then the plot, then the hunt, with a search that had been set aside taken up again; after twenty working minutes it lets the crossing go with what there is.
58. *The base is not a place to leave a pocket standing.* A pocket at the base comes down when it is left in the morning; a block on the stash chest's lid or on the bed is dug off before opening or sleeping; a sleep is judged by the clock even when mineflayer times out waiting for its event, and a refusal the server names is logged.
